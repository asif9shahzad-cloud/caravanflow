Caravan Flow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Caravan Flow ("the game") is made by Asif Shahzad, an independent developer in Qatar.

1. Information we collect

The game does not ask for your real name, email address or phone number, and there is no account of our own and no advertising. You can choose a player name (nickname) inside the game, which is shown on the public leaderboard. The game uses Google Firebase for its leaderboards, game statistics and country teams, and Google Play Games if you are signed in to it, as described below. We do not sell or share your data with other companies.

2. Leaderboards

The game shows a public player leaderboard. To build it, the app stores online (in Google Firebase, in Doha, Qatar) one record per player: a random anonymous player ID, the player name and profile picture you chose in the game (or a random name such as “Falcon123” and the default camel picture), your country, the highest level you have reached, the week in which it last went up, and how many new levels you won this month and last month. Your player name, picture, country flag, highest level and monthly score are shown to other players on the leaderboard. The top 3 players of each month are kept in a “Hall of fame” list (name, picture, country and monthly score), so please do not use your real name or personal details as your player name. You can change your player name at any time in the game’s Profile screen. These records are kept while the game is offered and are used only for the leaderboard.

The game also uses Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, your Play Games player name, avatar and your highest level reached are sent to Google and can be shown on Google's own Play Games leaderboard for this game. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive your email address or other account details. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility settings in the Google Play Games app.

3. Game statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To find out which levels are too hard and how to improve the game, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Firebase Analytics: for example when a level is started, won or failed and its number, when a booster is used, when the leaderboard is opened, the language you play in and the highest level reached. Firebase also records basic technical information such as device model, Android version, app version, approximate country (derived by Google from the IP address) and a random app-instance identifier. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or advertising ID. This data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and is used only for analytics. Firebase keeps this data for a limited period (by default 2 months for detailed event data) and then deletes it.

4. Country teams

Every player automatically plays for their country's team. To build the country ranking, the app stores online (in Google Firebase, in Doha, Qatar) one record per player: a random anonymous player ID, your country and the highest level you have reached. Your country is taken from your SIM card or mobile network, or from your phone's region setting. Each country's team shows only its total levels and number of players; no names are shown. These records are kept while the game is offered and are used only for the country ranking.

5. Notifications and updates

If you allow notifications, the game uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to send occasional game news, such as new levels, events and updates. For this, Firebase gives your device a random notification token; we do not receive your name, phone number or contacts. You can turn news off at any time in the game’s Settings or in your phone’s settings. The game also asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can show an “Update available” message; this is handled by Google Play.

6. Events

The game has optional events (Camel Race, Sandstorm Quest and Caravan Arena) where you play against other players. When you join one, the app stores online (in Google Firebase, in Doha, Qatar) one record for that event round: a random anonymous player ID, your player name and picture, your country, your group number, your score in the event and the times you joined and last played. Other players in the same group can see your name, picture, country flag and score. These records are used only to run the events. Daily Gifts, Oasis Pass and Lost City Mission are kept only on your device.

7. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, coins, lives, boosters, settings, and the player name and picture you choose) is stored on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

8. Internet use

The game can be played offline. When the device is online, the game may download its text fonts from Google Fonts and connect to Google Play Games and Google Firebase (leaderboards, events, analytics, country teams and notifications). Google may receive your device's IP address as part of those standard requests; we do not receive or store it.

9. In-app purchases and ads

The current version has no ads and no real-money purchases. If these are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first and the Google Play data-safety section will be updated accordingly.

10. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or to ask for your leaderboard or country-team record to be deleted: asif9shahzad@gmail.com
