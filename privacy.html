Caravan Flow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Caravan Flow ("the game") is made by Asif Shahzad, an independent developer in Qatar.

1. Information we collect

The game does not ask for, collect, or share any personal information itself. There is no account of our own, no advertising and no analytics.

2. Google Play Games (leaderboards)

The game uses Google Play Games Services to show a public leaderboard. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, your Play Games player name, avatar and your highest level reached are sent to Google and shown on the game's leaderboard to other players. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy); we do not receive your email address or other account details. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility settings in the Google Play Games app.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, coins, lives, boosters, settings and the player name you choose) is stored on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

4. Internet use

The game can be played offline. When the device is online, the game may download its text fonts from Google Fonts and connect to Google Play Games. Google may receive your device's IP address as part of those standard requests; we do not receive or store it.

5. In-app purchases and ads

The current version has no ads and no real-money purchases. If these are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first and the Google Play data-safety section will be updated accordingly.

6. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: asif9shahzad@gmail.com
